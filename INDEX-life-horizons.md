## Questions to browse by

### What counted as success?

Look for:

- security
- respectability
- promotion
- marriage
- household formation
- children
- education
- property
- independence
- old-age security

### What counted as failure?

Look for:

- unemployment
- debt
- dependence
- downward mobility
- inability to establish a household
- family breakdown
- loss of reputation

### What future was imaginable?

Each future entry should distinguish:

1. realistic paths
2. difficult but imaginable paths
3. paths effectively outside the practical horizon

## The core comparison

The repository now has two linked historical questions:

> **What could this person materially do?**

and

> **What did this person believe they were trying to become?**

Neither question is complete without the other.
