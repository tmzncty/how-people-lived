## 6. What did the pandemic do to ordinary life?

Start with:

- [Pandemic Everyday-Life Index](INDEX-pandemic.md)

- home becoming school/work infrastructure;

## 9. How can we reconstruct real life sequences empirically?

Start with:

- [Life-History Survey Guide](sources/life-history-survey-guide.md)

## 14. The repository's full model

The project now asks at least these linked questions:

1. **What could this person materially do?**
2. **What future could this person realistically imagine?**
3. **Which historical break arrived during this person's life?**
4. **How many separate projects did adulthood require?**
5. **Who financed and performed the labor behind those projects?**
6. **What age did each transition occur?**
7. **How did the answer differ for urban/rural, women/men, migrants/non-migrants and different family resources?**
8. **Which technologies were learned as novelties, and which were already background infrastructure?**
9. **Which memories and artifacts are about to disappear if nobody preserves them?**

That is the working definition of an atlas of ordinary life.
