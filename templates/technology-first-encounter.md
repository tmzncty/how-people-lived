# Technology First-Encounter Record

Use this template for one person's first meaningful encounter with a technology.

The purpose is not to prove a national adoption date.

It is to preserve **when a technology entered one life**.

## What kind of “first” was this?

Choose or explain:

- first time ever seeing it;
- first time using someone else's;
- first school/work access;
- first household ownership;
- first personal ownership;
- first time it became routine;
- first time it replaced an older method.

These are different milestones.

## Before the technology

How was the relevant task done before?

Examples:

- food kept cool with daily shopping / ice;
- clothes washed by hand;
- calls made through a public or neighbor's telephone;
- information looked up in books;
- Internet accessed only at school or an Internet café;
- directions written down or printed;
- school essays started from a blank page rather than an AI dialogue.

## The encounter

Describe:

- where the device/service was;
- who owned or controlled it;
- what the person first used it for;
- whether anyone taught them;
- what felt difficult, exciting, expensive, strange or disappointing;
- whether the first use immediately mattered.

## What changed afterward?

Which routines changed?

- frequency of shopping;
- time spent on housework;
- who could be contacted;
- waiting time;
- travel planning;
- schoolwork;
- work;
- entertainment;
- privacy;
- family relationships;
- sleep / daily schedule;
- ability to move away from home;
- ability to delegate cognitive work.

## What did not change immediately?

Avoid assuming total replacement.

Did the older method coexist?

Examples:

- washing machine but hand rinsing;
- mobile phone but no mobile Internet;
- computer but Internet only through dial-up;
- smartphone but PC still required for schoolwork;
- AI available but not trusted for important tasks.

## Privacy and copyright check

Before publishing:

- remove phone numbers;
- remove home addresses;
- remove account identifiers;
- redact QR codes / barcodes where necessary;
- obtain permission before publishing another person's private messages or identifiable photo;
- do not upload copyrighted manuals/books wholesale when a citation or small excerpt is sufficient.

## Confidence

Mark the record:

- **contemporary documented** — dated evidence survives;
- **retrospective, high confidence** — person remembers date/context clearly and corroborating evidence exists;
- **retrospective, approximate** — likely year or age only;
- **uncertain** — memory is incomplete or conflicting.

## One-sentence historical meaning

Finish with:

> Before this encounter, ________. After it became routine, ________.

That sentence is often the most useful part of the record.
