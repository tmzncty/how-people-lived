# China generations by domain

The cohort notes follow one birth group through time.

This directory asks the reverse question:

> **How did one part of life change across several cohorts?**

Current comparisons:

- [Education: from scarce access to mass differentiation to AI-assisted learning](education.md)
- [Work: from placement to career management to AI-augmented competence](work.md)
- [Housing: from scarce assigned space to family capital and market choice](housing.md)
- [Information: from delayed contact to continuous reachability to generated answers](information.md)
- [Family and care: from household labor to child investment and multi-generation planning](family-and-care.md)

These files should always preserve urban/rural and class differences where evidence allows.

They are comparative scaffolds, not universal histories.
